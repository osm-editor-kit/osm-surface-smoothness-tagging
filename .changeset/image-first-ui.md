Photos first, in two steps: a grid of surface photos (the usual surfaces, the rest behind "More"), then the smoothness reference photos for that surface (or every level with its emoji where there are none). A chosen step becomes one compact row with its photo. New `mode('text')` shows plain inputs instead, and the `optionLabel` adapter lets the host pass translated value labels.
