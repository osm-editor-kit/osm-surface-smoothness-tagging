Photos first: two tiles side by side, the surface and its smoothness (photo and label, or an empty frame while not set). Clicking a tile opens its picker below: all surface photos first, then the smoothness photos for that surface (or every level with its emoji where there are none). Below the photos, a label | input row takes any value, also one without a photo; the new `options` adapter adds the host's values to its suggestions, and `optionLabel` lets the host pass translated value labels.
